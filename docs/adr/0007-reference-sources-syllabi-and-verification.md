# Reference documents, syllabi and verification

## Reference documents

A reference document is the umbrella concept for every knowledge source: statutes, standards, codes, manuals and guidelines. It has a stable identity and specific editions. Law-specific properties such as amendments and effective dates belong to the matching document kind, so the real range of sources is covered without forcing the statute model on every source.

We start with manually approved document editions and fragment links, and from the start keep sources, versions and editorial decisions. Automated parsing, comparison and consolidation grow gradually and may prepare proposals; a full legal consolidation engine does not block launching study.

Displaying content and downloading the file are separate permissions set by editors. A document may expose full content, or only a description, references and a link to the external source. Assignment to a path does not grant file download. An approved PDF may be published before the structured content exists; it then opens as a PDF, optionally at a given page, and navigation by article, paragraph and other units follows once the structure is prepared.

## Fragments and question links

A question may point at specific document units, such as an article, paragraph, point or table. A fragment is identified by document and structural address, and its content is versioned independently of the address. Moves and renumbering need an explicit link; questions are never repointed automatically. This keeps reference identity and finds the questions affected by a change to a specific fragment.

Links are a direct basis for the answer, a supporting source or a topical relation. Editors mark which links matter for correctness. A change to an essential basis sends the question back for verification; a topical link alone never invalidates verification. Links also drive navigation from a question to its source and from a source to its questions. A current answer basis opens the exact fragment of the edition relevant to the study context; a historical answer opens the source version used for its verification, with the edition clearly labelled. From a fragment the user can reach related questions on the active path, within the document's content availability.

Editors may decide once that a change between two specific fragment versions does not affect the correctness of linked questions. The decision, its author and its reasoning are kept and apply to every relevant path and syllabus. A small textual difference is never treated as insignificant automatically, because meaning decides.

## Official syllabus and our interpretation

We archive the official syllabus files and separately maintain our structured interpretation of them. Fixing a parser bug changes our interpretation, not the official edition. When a chamber publishes revised material, we keep the new source and its link to the earlier one. This shows whether source requirements changed or we fixed our own mapping, and preserves data provenance.

## Verification evidence

Verification applies to a specific content revision, the relevant source versions and the scope it was checked for, with author and reasoning. Questions based on regulations require the relevant sources; practical oral questions may be verified differently. Closing an editorial work item does not verify anything.

One verification can hold across many paths and syllabi when every essential source fragment has the same unchanged wording. We verify a question revision against specific source content; compliance with a syllabus follows from comparing the syllabus requirements with that evidence. A new syllabus alone does not require re-verifying unchanged questions.

## Applicable syllabus

The user's target exam session and the confirmed material scope are separate. If the update to a new syllabus is not ready, the user may keep studying from the last confirmed set, with a clear notice of the applicable syllabus and of what is not yet confirmed for the new one. Access to study is kept without presenting the whole set as checked for the new session.
