# Chamber directory and qualification process guide

V2 keeps the "My chamber" directory with contact details relevant to the user. Directory data is content that a future Community may correct through the shared editorial process rather than closed admin configuration. This does not mean launching full Community in the first slice.

The guide covers the whole formal path to a licence: documents, the application, the following stages and the exam. A timeline shows the current position in the calendar and upcoming stages, accompanied by a personal checklist of formalities. The public preview of PIIB/IARP dates stays and gains process context.

The schedule holds shared stages and dates specific to a district chamber and session. Shared data is maintained once, and differences are attached to the matching scope rather than copying the process per path. The user sees one matching timeline.

Calendar position and completion of formalities are separate. The user ticks their own checklist; a passed deadline never marks an action, such as submitting the application, as done. The timeline shows the current stage and upcoming dates regardless of declared completion.

Dates and requirements keep their official source and the date last checked. Community proposals need approval. A date that is not confirmed is shown as such, never extrapolated from the previous season. Approved corrections update the shared data of the public page and the user's panel.

Matching local dates requires a district chamber; until then the shared information is shown. Changing the target session keeps the earlier checklist's history and prepares a checklist for the new goal, without carrying over every ticked item, such as an application for the previous season.
